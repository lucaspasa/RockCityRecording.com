About

Who We Are

Rock City Road is a premium boutique recording studio for artists who want to create records with depth, character, and lasting impact.

More than a place to record, it’s an immersive environment where distractions fall away and the focus returns to the music. Here, artists can stop thinking about the process and simply play.

Our philosophy is artist-first: the song comes first. We capture the emotion, chemistry, imperfections, and spontaneous moments that give music its soul. Rather than chasing perfection we chase authenticity. We preserve what feels real—because the most powerful records feel alive.

Set on ten secluded acres in New York’s Taconic Hills, Rock City Road pairs a spacious, acoustically rich live room with a calm, natural setting designed for deep creative work. It’s a destination where artists can fully commit to their vision.

Whether you’re recording a solo project, a full band, or spoken-word work, our goal is the same: to help you create something honest, immersive, and unmistakably yours—something that lasts.

Our Owner

Owner David Rinaldi’s vision grew out of necessity. Over years of playing and recording music, notably the bass player for alt pop band Even The Odd, he built close relationships with fellow musicians and began opening his home to help them track their projects. Sessions took place in the living room of his log cabin, where the natural acoustics of the wood created a surprisingly warm and desirable sound.

While the results were strong, it became clear that something more was needed. To better serve the music—and to separate recording from everyday family life—David set out to build a dedicated live room. That decision became the foundation for Rock City Road as it exists today: a purpose-built space designed to capture great performances without losing the comfort and authenticity that started it all.

David’s approach is also shaped by his broader experience in the recording world. He has worked with producer Mitch Easter, collaborated with engineer Keith Cleversley, and spent time at Abbey Road Studios—experiences that inform both his technical perspective and his commitment to capturing performances that feel timeless and true.

Our Sound

Rock City Road was designed around a simple idea: musicians give their best performances in rooms that inspire them.

Our live room is intentionally alive—not sterile. Rather than eliminating every reflection, we’ve created a space that’s spacious, natural, and controlled, allowing instruments and voices to breathe while remaining focused and mix-ready. It’s a room that encourages musicians to play together, feed off one another’s energy, and capture performances that feel authentic.

Every detail has been considered with the artist in mind, from personalized headphone mixes and comfortable lighting to quiet climate control that keeps the focus where it belongs: on the music. Two isolation booths provide flexibility when needed, including a dedicated booth optimized for vocals and spoken-word recording, while the control room offers a comfortable environment for listening, collaboration, and critical decision-making.

For artists traveling from outside the area, Rock City Road also offers overnight accommodations. Our green room converts into a private studio apartment that comfortably sleeps up to three guests, with additional lodging options available upon request.

The result is more than a recording space—it’s an environment designed to help artists relax, connect, and create performances worth keeping.
